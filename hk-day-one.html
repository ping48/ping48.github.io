Getting in from the airport

  • Take a taxi from the rank, or an Uber. About HK$340–380 for the two of you with tolls and bags, roughly 30 minutes on empty Sunday roads. Sunrise is 06:09, so sit on the left for the Tsing Ma Bridge, the container port and the Kowloon skyline before the Western Harbour Tunnel drops you a few blocks from the hotel. Show the driver "Best Western Plus, 308 Des Voeux Road West, Sai Ying Pun".
  • Uber pickup: Arrivals Hall A meets at Car Park 4, Hall B at Car Park 1. UberX runs about HK$353, Uber Taxi about HK$336 with an upfront price. Uber is in a licensing transition this year but still works normally.
  • Skip DiDi in Hong Kong. It only dispatches the same red taxis and often rejects foreign cards. Install it anyway: it is the ride app on the mainland, where Uber does not exist, and it lives inside Alipay and WeChat.
  • Cheaper alternative, Airport Express. Runs from 05:54, every 10 minutes, 24 minutes to Hong Kong Station. HK$120 on Octopus, or tap a contactless Visa or Mastercard at the gate. Then a 10-minute taxi, or the Island line two stops to Sai Ying Pun exit B3. Saves about HK$100 for two but most of the ride is in tunnels.
  • Octopus. iPhone: Wallet, plus, Transit Card, Hong Kong, "Octopus for Tourists". A US card works there. HK$50 deposit, HK$100 minimum load. Or buy a physical card at the MTR counter in arrivals. You need it for the tram, ferry and MTR all day.
  • Drop bags at the hotel. Storage is free and now in the carpark. Ask about early check-in; reviews mention rooms ready around noon.

The day

  1. 07:15

    Sun Hing dim sum

    8 Smithfield Rd, Kennedy Town. 15-minute walk west of the hotel, or one tram stop.

    Open since 03:00 every day (03:00–16:00). Grab trays straight off the steamer carts, share a table with old-timers and students, pay cash. Salted-egg lava buns, quail-egg siu mai, fried milk.

  2. 08:15

    Kennedy Town waterfront

    Belcher Bay Promenade, then Alex's court at the end of Sai Ning St, then Instagram Pier.

    Alex's basketball court is the Kennedy Town Temporary Recreation Ground, right on the water. Instagram Pier is the old cargo working area next door. Expect swells and locals doing tai chi. Duck into a café if a shower rolls through.

  3. 09:15

    Ding-ding tram east to Central

    Kennedy Town terminus. About 35 minutes. HK$3 flat, tap Octopus on exit.

    Upper deck, front seats. The 1904 double-decker tram is the most Hong Kong way to cross the island.

  4. 10:00

    Sunday in Central

    Chater Road, Statue Square, under the HSBC building.

    Chater Road closes to cars on Sundays and thousands of Filipina domestic helpers turn the financial district into a picnic. Only happens on Sunday. Part of the Statue Square garden is fenced for underpass works this year.

    Sunday only
  5. 10:20

    Mid-Levels Escalator, uphill

    Starts at Cochrane St by Central Market. Runs downhill 06:00–10:00, then switches to uphill at 10:20.

    Alex's tip: hop off wherever looks good, eat, hop back on. Stops worth the detour: Cheung Hing Kee pan-fried buns at 48 Lyndhurst Terrace right beside the escalator, Man Mo Temple on Hollywood Road (08:00–18:00), Tai Kwun, the former police station and prison turned arts complex (10:00–23:00, free), and PMQ.

  6. 12:30

    Lunch at Yat Lok

    34–38 Stanley St, Central. Sunday 10:00–17:30.

    One Michelin star, roast goose leg on rice for about HK$100 at a formica table. This is exactly Alex's point about Hong Kong Michelin. The queue moves fast.

    Michelin, cheap
  7. 13:45

    Star Ferry to Tsim Sha Tsui

    Central Pier 7. Nine minutes, about HK$5–6.5 on Octopus.

    The skyline from the water. Upper deck for the view, lower deck for the engine smell and the 1950s vibe.

  8. 14:15

    Alex's dumplings, then MTR to Prince Edward

    Cheung Hing Kee, Shop 6A, 48 Lock Rd, TST. 09:00–21:00 daily.

    Skip this if you already ate at the Lyndhurst Terrace branch. Then Tsuen Wan line from TST to Prince Edward, about 10 minutes.

  9. 15:00

    Prince Edward with Danny

    Flower Market Rd, Yuen Po St Bird Garden (to 20:00), Goldfish Market on Tung Choi St (10:00–20:00).

    Eat at Kam Wah Café, 47 Bute St, for the ice-fire pineapple bun and a silk-stocking milk tea, open 06:30 to late. For a proper sit-down, One Dim Sum at 209A Tung Choi St, Sunday 08:30–23:00. If Danny would rather do dinner, run this block at 18:30 and move the Monster Building up to 15:00.

  10. 16:45

    Monster Building, if you still have legs

    Yick Cheong Building, Quarry Bay MTR exit A. About 30 minutes from Prince Edward via Admiralty.

    Alex's Transformers building. Photography inside the courtyard is officially banned now, so look from the street and keep it quiet, it is people's homes. Otherwise it goes on Monday.

    Optional
  11. 18:00

    Hotel, shower, one-hour nap

    Island line to Sai Ying Pun.

    One hour maximum. Stay up until about 22:30 and the jet lag is mostly done by Monday.

  12. 19:30

    Dinner in the neighbourhood

    Third Street and High Street, five minutes from the hotel.

    Sai Ying Pun's restaurant strip. Wander and pick what looks good.

  13. 21:00

    Ping Pong 129

    129 Second St, lower ground of Nam Cheong House. Five-minute walk from the hotel. Sunday 17:00–24:00.

    Alex's ex-ping-pong-hall gin bar. No minimum spend on Sundays. It is closed on Mondays, so it has to be tonight.

    Tonight or never

Monday, the sunny day

Peak Tram early

Ride 07:30–09:00 to skip the 20–60 minute queues. Runs 07:00–22:00, HK$88 return. Walk the Lugard Road loop at the top.

Stanley, Alex's chill south side

Citybus 6X or 260 from Exchange Square, about 45 minutes. Market, waterfront, beach. Alternative: Dragon's Back hike (bus 9 from Shau Kei Wan) then Shek O beach.

Evening

Symphony of Lights at 20:00 from the TST promenade (ten minutes, free, mildly underwhelming), Temple Street night market after, then Lan Kwai Fong, Alex's bar strip. A cab back to Sai Ying Pun is 15 minutes.

Do today, before you fly

  • Book Tuesday's train to Shenzhen now. West Kowloon to Futian takes 14 minutes. Sales open 15 days out so 15 September is already on sale. Use the MTR High Speed Rail site, the 12306 app, or Trip.com; US Visa and Mastercard work. E-ticket, scan your passport at the gate. Shane got stranded buying last minute. Arrive an hour early.
  • Test WeChat Pay in Hong Kong on Sunday so the Shenzhen drone order works.
  • Skip Keeta drones in Hong Kong. The only public route is Science Park to Ma On Shan promenade, an hour away in the New Territories. Save it for Shenzhen.
  • Install the Michelin Guide app (Alex's tip), MyObservatory, and an eSIM.
